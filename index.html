About MetroPulse

Architecture, safety ethics, and offline design rationale.

🚇 Problem Statement PS-07: "Every Complaint Heard"

In underground metro networks, deep tunnels, and heavily crowded platforms, passenger mobile connectivity is frequently interrupted or completely absent. Traditional cloud-dependent complaint reporting systems fail silently, resulting in lost safety hazards and delayed assistance.

MetroPulse is an offline-first Passenger Complaint Intelligence and Management System engineered to provide zero-latency incident intake, deterministic heuristic safety triage, and resilient local data persistence—requiring zero external internet connectivity or background neural network model execution.

🏗️ Architecture & Offline Implementation
  • 100% Zero-Cloud Dependency: Runs completely within the browser sandbox using vanilla HTML5, CSS3, and modern ECMAScript.
  • Deterministic Safety-First Triage: Evaluates dual-factor incident features. Serious hazards (unconscious passenger, fire, person on tracks) bypass ordinary categories.
  • Negation-Aware Regular Expressions: Distinguishes explicit absences (e.g. "no fire or smoke") from active emergencies to avoid false alarms.
  • Isolated Client-Side Persistence: Uses localStorage to preserve submissions across browser restarts without server infrastructure.
  • Zero-Battery Overhead: Requires no CPU-heavy background LLM inference, ensuring operational readiness during power emergencies.
⚖️ Responsible Transit AI Ethics & Boundaries
  • Automated Suggestions Only: Triage classifications and recommended responses are decision-support aids; station personnel retain dispatch authority.
  • Honest Transmission Boundary: The app explicitly clarifies that local storage saves complaints to this machine/browser and does not automatically dial 911 or dispatch train operators.
  • Privacy by Default: Zero tracking cookies, third-party analytics, or mandatory personal identifiers. Commuters may submit anonymously.
  • Data Integrity: Benchmark sample records are visibly distinguished from live passenger submissions and can be cleared with one click.
🛡️ Safety Disclaimer: MetroPulse is an operational management and triage decision-support prototype. It does not replace direct emergency equipment (such as emergency stop buttons, passenger alarm handles, or emergency station phones).
